# mAIth Vision

## Mission

Make difficult thinking addictive, measurable, trustworthy, and accessible to anyone.

## Vision

Build a living map of human knowledge where anyone can move from first principles to the frontier through challenges, exploration, competition, and intelligent guidance.

mAIth begins with mathematics and quantitative reasoning, but its long-term subject is mastery itself: understanding difficult ideas, connecting them across domains, proving that understanding through increasingly authentic work, and continuously extending the map as knowledge advances.

## Product promise

**Learn it. Prove it. Connect it. Master it.**

The existing product line remains valid:

> Solve mAIth = Solve everything.

The broader interpretation is intentional. “mAIth” is not limited to arithmetic or school mathematics; it represents structured reasoning across mathematics, science, engineering, computing, finance, and adjacent disciplines.

## Core principles

### 1. Mastery over volume

Question count is a means, not the outcome. A larger bank is useful only when it creates better coverage, better variation, stronger diagnostics, or more convincing evidence of understanding.

### 2. Concepts before screens

The durable product model is a graph of concepts, prerequisites, applications, evidence, and mastery. Quiz, Learn, Formulas, Glossary, MasterMinds, Bonafides, Vault, and future experiences are different views onto that shared model.

### 3. Evidence over vibes

A learner should be able to see why mAIth believes a concept is mastered, weak, stale, or untested. Scores should be traceable to attempts, recency, difficulty, and demonstrated reasoning rather than opaque gamification.

### 4. First principles to frontier

The same system should support a beginner learning a definition and an expert testing current state-of-the-art material. Difficulty is a progression through depth and authenticity, not merely more obscure trivia.

### 5. Trustworthy by construction

Current, contested, quantitative, and frontier claims require provenance and freshness. Structural integrity, editorial quality, localization quality, and source review remain independent release gates.

### 6. Interaction beats passive explanation

Whenever a concept can be manipulated, simulated, derived, visualized, coded, estimated, compared, or argued, mAIth should eventually prefer that over passive reading.

### 7. AI should teach, not merely answer

The mAIth mentor should diagnose misconceptions, ask useful questions, generate targeted practice, explain from the learner’s current level, and preserve productive struggle. It should not default to revealing solutions.

### 8. Multilingual means equivalent

A language is supported only to the extent that learners can meaningfully use the product in it. Translation coverage, mathematical fidelity, and progression parity matter more than nominal locale count.

### 9. Competition should reward learning

Streaks, ranks, leagues, challenges, and multiplayer mechanics should reinforce persistence, breadth, depth, and improvement rather than exploit answer spam or memorization.

### 10. The frontier stays alive

mAIth should be able to revise what it teaches as knowledge changes. SOTA material, research notes, experiments, and reviewed claims are versioned knowledge, not frozen trivia.

## Product system

The long-term product spine is:

**Human Knowledge Graph → Learner Model → Adaptive Engine → Challenge Engine → Socratic Mentor → Interactive Labs → Social Competition → Verified Mastery**

### Human Knowledge Graph

Represents concepts, prerequisite relationships, applications, formulas, people, disciplines, learning resources, evidence, and frontier claims.

### Learner Model

Tracks concept-level evidence: accuracy, difficulty, recency, hints, confidence, repeated misconceptions, unresolved dependencies, and demonstrated transfer.

### Adaptive Engine

Selects the next best challenge or explanation based on the learner’s goals and current evidence rather than topic popularity alone.

### Challenge Engine

Expands beyond four-option questions over time into numeric response, ordering, derivation, proof steps, code, estimation, diagrams, simulations, and open reasoning.

### Socratic Mentor

Uses the same canonical content and learner state to provide grounded, context-aware guidance without bypassing the learning objective.

### Interactive Labs

Turns abstract concepts into manipulable systems: graphs, vectors, circuits, orbital models, probability experiments, algorithms, financial models, and more.

### Social Competition

Supports topic duels, daily global challenges, teams, leagues, cooperative problems, and asynchronous competition tied to real mastery evidence.

### Verified Mastery

Surfaces durable capability records: what was demonstrated, at what difficulty, how recently, and through what kinds of evidence.

## Existing surfaces in the vision

- **Test Time** — fast challenge and diagnostic loop.
- **Learn** — structured conceptual exploration.
- **Formulas** — mathematical objects, derivations, and applications.
- **Glossary** — atomic concepts and terminology.
- **MasterMinds** — ideas through the people who advanced them.
- **Bonafides** — rigorous professional/course pathways.
- **Vault** — evidence-aware historical, classified, disputed, or unusual material with explicit epistemic boundaries.
- **Profile** — learner model and mastery map.
- **Leaderboard** — social proof, competition, and improvement.
- **SOTA** — living frontier material with provenance and review dates.
- **mAIth Research** — reproducible experiments and technical investigations that can feed future learning content.

## Strategic horizons

### Foundation

Make the corpus structurally sound, balanced, sourced, localized, fast, and easy to extend.

### Mastery

Introduce canonical concept relationships and concept-level learner evidence.

### Intelligence

Add diagnostics, adaptive sequencing, misconception models, and a grounded Socratic mentor.

### Interaction

Add non-multiple-choice assessment and interactive visual/simulation-based learning.

### Network

Add social challenges, richer competition, teams, and collaborative learning.

### Institution

Add teacher, mentor, classroom, cohort, and organization workflows built on the same mastery model.

### Frontier

Treat mAIth as a living knowledge system that integrates reviewed SOTA material and reproducible research.

## Near-term rule

Do not jump to flashy AI or multiplayer features before the mastery substrate exists.

The immediate sequence is:

1. finish current content-quality and taxonomy work;
2. define a minimal concept graph that coexists with the canonical topic registry;
3. record concept-level evidence from existing quiz sessions;
4. surface that evidence in Profile;
5. use it to drive the first adaptive practice loop;
6. only then layer mentor and interactive experiences on top.

This preserves the rigor already built into mAIth while creating a path from a strong quiz product to a universal system for intellectual mastery.
